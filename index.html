The Greek & Western column follows the figures covered in Daniel N. Robinson’s course The Great Ideas of Philosophy (2nd ed.), which includes historians, scientists and political writers who shaped Western thought; the Indian and Chinese columns are an editorial selection of the most widely taught thinkers. Texts marked “compiled” were assembled by disciples or later followers; “attrib.” marks traditional attributions that modern scholars doubt. “trad.” marks traditional dates. Dates before about 300 CE are approximate, and some early figures (Kapila, Laozi, Yajnavalkya) are traditional attributions whose historical existence is debated. Entries in amber with no person attached are anonymous or collective texts. Islamic and Jewish thinkers of the medieval period are placed in the Western column because they worked in the Greek philosophical lineage.
