This is an editorial selection of the thinkers and texts most widely taught and cited in each tradition. Dates before about 300 CE are approximate, and some early figures (Kapila, Laozi, Yajnavalkya) are traditional attributions whose historical existence is debated. Entries in amber with no person attached are anonymous or collective texts. Islamic and Jewish thinkers of the medieval period are placed in the Western column because they worked in the Greek philosophical lineage.
